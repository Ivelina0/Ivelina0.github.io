Gave a joint talk with Norberto in one of Professor Claudia Garetto's seminars for the [History of Mathematics (MTH6003U)](https://curriculum.qmul.ac.uk/subject/2026/MTH6003U) module at QMUL, on lessons from the History of Maths summer projects and from doing research. My part was about doing research in the age of AI: use AI as a tool that helps you and enhances your research, but not as something you can trust to be 100% accurate, so always check what it gives you.
