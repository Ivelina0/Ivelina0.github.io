  Neural network approximation of option price derivatives. We proved the existence of neural networks that approximate option pricing maps together with their derivatives. Working in anisotropic Sobolev spaces, where we adapted a neural network architecture to learn pricing maps and show that the derivatives (the Greeks) can be taken and are approximated as well.
